# Panasonic-PowerShell-Cmdlets
Panasonic PS Cmdlets

Panasonic has 3 different libraries for their PS cmdlets.

1) Panasonic BIOS Commandlets (Configure and customer Panasonic BIOS through PS.)
2) Panasonic PC Hub Configuraiton Commandlets (Configure the PC Hub to make the most out of your Panasonic device. PC Hub PS cmdlets allows you to configure button mapping, day night mode, Power on AC settings, and many others allowing you to have a consistent experience across all your devices.)
3) Panasonic Update Commandlets (Allows you to keep you Panasonic devices updated with the latest drivers.)
